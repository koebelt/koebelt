`ProjectCard` is the portfolio grid tile. Hover turns the media hairline and the title lime and fills the corner arrow.

```jsx
<ProjectCard title="Atelier Vasse" year="2025" summary="Identity and site for a Lyon furniture studio."
  tags={["Brand identity","Web"]} href="/work/atelier-vasse" />
```

Lay them out in a 2-column grid at `--grid-gap`; use `ratio="3/4"` for a portrait row to break rhythm.
